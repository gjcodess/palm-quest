PALMQuest

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy explains how PALMQuest, the Coconut Palm Crackers Virtual Laboratory, handles information in its Android app (package com.gjcodess.palmquest) and on palm-quest.vercel.app. PALMQuest is developed by gjcodes and the PALMQuest project team.

Your learning progress stays on your device. PALMQuest does not require an online account or send your student name, answers, scores, or saved progress to the development team.

Information stored on your device

PALMQuest uses local app or browser storage to remember:

  • The student name you enter, used to personalize the learning experience. You can use a nickname instead of your real name.
  • Learning progress, stages and workstation steps, assessment answers and scores, and activity timer state, so you can continue where you left off.
  • Preferences such as whether sound is muted.

The app does not provide cloud accounts, a server-side student database, advertisements, or in-app analytics tracking. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location.

Website and optional Creative Studio

The website is hosted by Vercel. When you visit the website or this policy, the hosting provider receives technical request information, such as your IP address, requested URL, and browser information, to deliver and secure the site. Hosting information is handled under Vercel's privacy policy. This is separate from learning progress stored locally by the game.

If you use the optional Creative Studio, edited captions and template selections are saved in your browser. Images you choose are processed locally in the open tab to generate graphics; the studio does not upload those images to our server. Replacement images are not retained by the studio after the tab is closed or reloaded. Exported files remain wherever you save them.

Sharing and external links

We do not sell your personal information or transmit your local learning records to advertisers or other organizations. If you choose to share a screenshot or exported image, the information visible in that file is shared with the recipient you select.

Optional links to the developer's portfolio or other websites open services with their own privacy practices. If you email us, your email provider and ours process the message, and we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your enquiry. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until it is reset or removed. Starting a new batch resets session progress but retains your student name and preferences. To remove all local app information on Android, open device Settings, select Apps → PALMQuest → Storage, and choose Clear storage or Clear data. On the website, clear site data for palm-quest.vercel.app in your browser. These actions remove saved progress.

Android or your device provider may back up app data according to your device and account settings. Manage or delete those backups through the relevant device or backup service. Files you export must be deleted separately from your downloads or chosen save location.

Privacy and support emails are retained only as needed to respond and resolve the enquiry, or where a legal obligation requires retention. You can ask us to delete your correspondence using the contact address below.

Security

The public website uses HTTPS. Local progress is stored in the app's or browser's storage and is not separately encrypted by PALMQuest. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to view it. Use your device's screen lock and appropriate access controls.

Children and learning

PALMQuest is an educational simulation. Students can use a nickname, and the game does not send student profiles or assessment records to us. A parent, guardian, or teacher can help a learner clear local information. If you believe personal information about a child was sent to us through a support message, contact us to request deletion.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if our data practices change. Any future feature that changes how information is collected or shared will be reflected in this policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the PALMQuest developer (gjcodes) at glennjoshuacorpus1@gmail.com.
